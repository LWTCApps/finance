Notifications

Choose who is emailed when a student submits a form. Make groups of addresses (for example Financial Aid Advisors, Program Administrators, a VA desk), then choose which groups hear about each form.

Email groups

Who is emailed for each form

A form with no setting of its own uses the default groups. Give a form its own groups when different people should hear about it. Choosing no groups means nobody is emailed for that form.

Staff emails never include the student’s name or ID. They say a submission arrived and point staff to the console, where access is controlled by role.
