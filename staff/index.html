Financial aid & assistance options

Please review the funding options below. Students are encouraged to apply for all options for which they may be eligible before the start of the term so that funding is in place by the drop/add deadline.

AFederal Student Aid (FAFSA)

Apply at studentaid.gov. The FAFSA determines eligibility for the Federal Pell Grant.

LWTC School Code: 013234

BCareerSource Southwest Florida

Tuition assistance and workforce grants (WIOA) for eligible job seekers in Southwest Florida.

careersourcesouthwestflorida.com

CCollier Community Foundation

Donor-established scholarships, including several for technical and vocational students, through one common online application.

Apply at: colliercf.org

Scan to apply on your phone
DLWTC Institutional Scholarships & Assistance

Institutional scholarships and Workforce Development Assistance (WDA) based on need, academic standing, and program requirements. Ask Financial Aid about current cycles.

Apply at: LWTC Internal Scholarship Application

Scan to apply on your phone
EFlorida Bright Futures Scholarship

Florida high school graduates who meet academic, test score, and service hour requirements — including Gold Seal Vocational and Gold Seal CAPE Scholars — Please provide to the financial aid office: award confirmation

FLearn 2 Earn Charitable Foundation

Scholarships plus mentorship and success coaching for students pursuing career, technical, and trade certifications.

Apply at: learn2earnscholarship.org

GVeterans Affairs (VA) Education Benefits

Veterans, service members, and eligible dependents may use VA education benefits

Please provide to the Financial Aid Office:

  • DD-214 (Certificate of Discharge from Active Duty)
  • Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from the VA
HFlorida Vocational Rehabilitation (VR)

Possible financial support and career services for individuals with physical, mental, or learning disabilities to prepare for, gain, or retain employment.

https://rehabworks.org/
