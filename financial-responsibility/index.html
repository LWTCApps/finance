Choose how you plan to pay for your program, then confirm you understand what’s due and when. It takes about five minutes.

Payment deadline: end of drop/add

All outstanding tuition, laboratory fees, and user fees must be paid in full, or covered by an approved funding source on file with the Financial Aid Office, by the end of the drop/add period for each term of enrollment.

Financial aid & assistance options

Review the options below. Apply for every one you may qualify for before the term starts, so your funding is in place by the drop/add deadline.

A

Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)

The FAFSA determines your eligibility for the Federal Pell Grant.

LWTC school code: 013234

Apply at studentaid.gov
B

CareerSource Southwest Florida

Tuition assistance and workforce grants (WIOA) for eligible job seekers in Southwest Florida.

Visit CareerSource SWFL
C

Collier Community Foundation

Donor-established scholarships, including several for technical and vocational students, through one common online application.

Apply at colliercf.org
D

LWTC institutional scholarships & assistance

Institutional scholarships and Workforce Development Assistance (WDA), based on need, academic standing, and program requirements. Ask Financial Aid about current cycles.

LWTC internal scholarship application
E

Florida Bright Futures Scholarship

For Florida high school graduates who meet academic, test score, and service hour requirements, including Gold Seal Vocational and Gold Seal CAPE Scholars.

Bring to Financial Aid: your award confirmation.

F

Learn 2 Earn Charitable Foundation

Scholarships plus mentorship and success coaching for students pursuing career, technical, and trade certifications.

Apply at learn2earnscholarship.org
G

Veterans Affairs (VA) education benefits

Veterans, service members, and eligible dependents may use VA education benefits.

Bring to Financial Aid:

  • DD-214 (Certificate of Discharge from Active Duty)
  • Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from the VA
H

Florida Vocational Rehabilitation (VR)

Possible financial support and career services for people with physical, mental, or learning disabilities to prepare for, gain, or retain employment.

Visit rehabworks.org
